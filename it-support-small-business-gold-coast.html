What most of them share is a need for IT that works reliably, a limited budget for technology and a preference for advice that is honest rather than sales-driven. That is exactly what we provide.

Many Gold Coast small businesses operate without any formal IT support — the owner resets the router when it drops, a technically minded staff member fixes the printer and the business runs on hope that nothing serious breaks. This works until it doesn’t — and when a hard drive fails, ransomware encrypts the accounting software or the business email stops delivering, the cost of not having IT support in place becomes very clear very quickly. A managed IT arrangement with Bcom is not expensive and covers the monitoring, maintenance and backup that prevents most of these events — and provides a fast response when they happen anyway.

For most Gold Coast small businesses, Microsoft 365 is the right starting point — business email with your own domain, Teams for internal communication, SharePoint for shared document storage and OneDrive for individual cloud storage. We set this up correctly, migrate from existing webmail or Gmail if needed, configure multi-factor authentication and ensure mobile devices for all staff are properly connected. Many Gold Coast small businesses are running Microsoft 365 accounts that were set up incorrectly — MFA not enabled, shared mailboxes configured as user accounts, no backup in place. We audit and fix these setups.

Cybersecurity for a Gold Coast small business does not need to be complex or expensive. The core requirements are: multi-factor authentication on all cloud accounts, email security filtering to block phishing and malware, automated cloud backup that is tested and verified, and basic staff awareness of phishing tactics. We implement these controls and the combination covers the vast majority of the security incidents we see in the Gold Coast small business sector.
