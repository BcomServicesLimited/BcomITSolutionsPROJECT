Business WiFi Services for Gold Coast Premises

New WiFi Installation

Site survey, coverage design and access point installation for Gold Coast offices, retail shops and hospitality venues. Ubiquiti UniFi, Aruba Instant On and TP-Link Omada systems installed and configured.

Network Segmentation & VLANs

Guest, staff and IoT devices on separate network segments — guest WiFi cannot access business systems, EFTPOS terminals or internal servers. Standard practice for any Gold Coast business handling payment data.

Site Survey & Coverage Design

We survey the premises before installation — walls, floors, interference sources and device density all mapped before a single access point is mounted. Correct placement eliminates dead zones.

WiFi Fault Diagnosis & Repair

Slow speeds, dead zones, authentication failures and dropped connections diagnosed and resolved on-site. Most Gold Coast WiFi faults are fixable without new hardware — configuration is usually the issue.

Multi-Site & Multi-Floor WiFi

Consistent WiFi across multiple floors or multiple Gold Coast locations — unified management, consistent SSID and seamless roaming between access points.

Hospitality & High-Density WiFi

WiFi for Gold Coast hotels, restaurants, cafes and retail venues where 50–200+ devices connect simultaneously. Access points and channel configuration optimised for device density rather than just coverage.
