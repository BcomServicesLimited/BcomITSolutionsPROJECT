Frequently Asked Questions — Managed Services & Telecoms

How quickly do you respond to IT issues?

For managed IT clients, we aim to respond to critical issues within 2 hours and non-critical issues within 4 business hours. For ad-hoc clients, we typically offer fast-turnaround or next-day on-site visits for most Gold Coast locations.

Do you support clients with multiple locations?

Yes. We support clients with multiple offices and sites across the Gold Coast and surrounding areas. Remote support is available for issues that do not require a physical visit.

Can you take over support from our existing IT provider?

Yes. We handle the transition from your existing provider, including documentation of your current systems, transfer of any managed services, and a full IT audit to identify any gaps or risks. The process is designed to be seamless with minimal disruption to your operations.
