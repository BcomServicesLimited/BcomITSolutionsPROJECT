VoIP Pricing for Gold Coast Businesses — What You Pay and What You Get

Our VoIP platform is priced simply — a monthly per-user fee and a one-time setup cost. No hidden call charges for standard Australian and New Zealand calls, no annual contracts, no per-minute billing surprises at the end of the month. Here is exactly what is included.

Monthly Fee

$25.00 + GST per user per month

Includes unlimited outbound calls to Australian and New Zealand landlines and mobiles (fair use policy applies). Inbound calls included. No per-minute charges for standard AUS and NZ calls. Month to month — no lock-in contract, cancel any time with standard notice.

One-Time Setup Fee

$100.00 + GST per user

Covers full system configuration, number porting or new number allocation, handset programming, Teams direct routing setup if required, AI feature configuration and staff onboarding. Paid once at installation — no ongoing setup or administration fees.

Handsets (Optional)

From approx. $350.00 per handset

Use your existing SIP-compatible desk phones at no additional hardware cost. If you need new handsets we supply quality SIP phones and certified Microsoft Teams phones (including Yealink Teams Phones) — quoted per device based on your requirements.

All pricing excludes GST. Fair use policy applies to unlimited call inclusions — standard business call volumes are covered. International calls outside Australia and New Zealand charged separately at standard rates. Call 07 3041 8993 for a full quote based on your team size and requirements.
