What Is a PABX and How Long Should One Last?

PABX stands for Private Automatic Branch Exchange — the on-site hardware unit that manages all internal and external phone calls for your business. Rather than every desk having its own phone line, the PABX connects to one or more external lines and routes calls internally between all extensions. It handles everything from transferring calls and setting up hunt groups to managing voicemail, auto-attendant and DDI numbers. A well-maintained PABX from a quality brand will run reliably for 10–15 years or more. The decision to repair, upgrade or replace depends on the age of the system, parts availability and whether the current capacity still fits your business.

Moving Your Gold Coast Office? Your PABX Needs a Plan

Relocating a PABX is not the same as relocating a PC. It is a hardware system with physical line connections, internal wiring, programmed configuration and sometimes ageing components that need careful handling. Businesses that move their PABX without planning often arrive at the new premises to discover missing cabling, incorrect line connections, lost programming or a system fault triggered by the move. We handle PABX relocations as a planned, documented job — not an afterthought on moving day.

Pre-Move Assessment

We visit your new Gold Coast premises before moving day — checking existing cabling, confirming the new location for the PABX cabinet, identifying what cabling needs to be run and noting anything that needs attention before the system arrives. You know exactly what is needed before a single cable is touched.

Professional Disconnection and Reinstallation

The PABX is professionally disconnected, all programming documented, the unit transported safely and reinstalled at the new Gold Coast premises. Lines reconnected, handsets programmed to the new layout, all features tested before the job is signed off.

Minimum Downtime

We plan the relocation around your move date so your Gold Coast business has phone service restored as quickly as possible. For businesses that cannot afford downtime we discuss same-day reinstallation options during the pre-move assessment.
