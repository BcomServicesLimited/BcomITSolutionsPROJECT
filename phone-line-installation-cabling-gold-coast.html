Phone Line Cabling vs Structured Data Cabling — Which Do You Need?

Phone Line Cabling

  • Analogue and digital phone lines for desk handsets
  • PABX, PBX and VoIP handset connections
  • Extension outlets for existing phone systems
  • Internal phone wiring between PBX and handset locations
  • This page — phone-line-installation-cabling-gold-coast.html

Structured Data Cabling

  • Cat5e and Cat6 cabling for computers and network devices
  • Patch panels, data outlets and server room cabling
  • Network switches and access point cabling runs
  • Suitable for both data and VoIP handsets on IP networks
  • See our data cabling Gold Coast page for this service

Phone line cabling refers to the internal wiring that connects analogue or digital phone handsets to a PBX, PABX or phone system — traditionally using two or four-core phone cable. Data cabling (structured cabling) uses Cat5e or Cat6 cable and is designed for computer network connections and IP-based devices. Many modern VoIP handsets actually connect over Cat6 data cable rather than traditional phone cable — so if you are installing a VoIP system, you may need data cabling rather than traditional phone cabling.
