How Our Remote Connection Works — Splashtop SOS

We use Splashtop SOS for all remote support sessions. It's designed specifically for on-demand support — not unattended access — which means your Gold Coast business machines are never accessible without your knowledge or permission.

01

You Download the App

Visit our support page and download the Splashtop SOS application. No installation — it runs directly without being installed on your machine. Takes under 60 seconds to be ready.

02

You Give Us Your 9-Digit Code

The app generates a unique 9-digit session code that changes every time it is opened. You read that code to us over the phone. We cannot connect to your machine without it — there is no stored access, no saved credentials, no backdoor.

03

Session Ends When You Close the App

Once the job is done, close the Splashtop SOS app. The session ends immediately and completely. No background processes, nothing left running, no way for us to reconnect until you open the app and give us a new code.
