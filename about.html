Gold Coast IT Support, Done Right

Bcom IT Solutions Pty Ltd (ABN 92 636 893 108) is a Gold Coast based IT support company built specifically for the local market. We work with small businesses and home users across Southport, Burleigh Heads, Robina, Nerang, Helensvale, Coomera, Palm Beach, Varsity Lakes, Coolangatta, Surfers Paradise and Broadbeach.

Our team of experienced technicians understands the unique challenges faced by Gold Coast residents and businesses — from the demands of a fast-growing city to the practical realities of running a small business in Queensland. We are not a national helpdesk or an interstate call centre. When you call us on 07 3041 8993, you speak to someone local who knows the Gold Coast and can actually help.

We cover the full spectrum of IT needs — computer repair, WiFi installation, managed IT support, business phone systems, VoIP, data cabling, and IT consulting. Whether it's a home laptop that won't start or a business network that needs rebuilding from the ground up, we treat every job with the same care and professionalism.
