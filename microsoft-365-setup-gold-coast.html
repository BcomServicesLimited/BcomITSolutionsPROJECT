Microsoft 365 Migration Services Gold Coast

Seamless transition from legacy systems to the cloud.

A Microsoft 365 migration moves your existing emails, files, and calendars from legacy servers or Google Workspace into Microsoft 365 with zero data loss and minimal downtime. We handle the entire transition for Gold Coast businesses, ensuring your team can keep working without interruption.

Moving to a new platform can be disruptive if not planned correctly. Our migration process is designed to be invisible to your staff. We perform the heavy lifting of data transfer in the background, typically over a weekend, so your team logs out on Friday and logs into their new Microsoft 365 environment on Monday morning.

We migrate from all major platforms, including on-premise Exchange servers, Google Workspace (G Suite), IMAP/POP3 webmail, and older versions of Office. Microsoft 365 Setup for Gold Coast Businesses — What We Actually Do

Microsoft 365 is the right starting point for most Gold Coast businesses — business email with your own domain, Teams for internal communication, SharePoint for shared document storage and OneDrive for cloud backup. The challenge is that it is frequently set up incorrectly — accounts created without multi-factor authentication enabled, shared mailboxes configured as user licences, no backup in place and mobile devices not connected. We see this regularly across Gold Coast businesses of all sizes and it is straightforward to fix once identified.

The most common setup we handle is a new Gold Coast business moving from a free Gmail or webmail account to Microsoft 365 Business — a business email address with your own domain, Teams for the team and SharePoint for shared files. We configure the Microsoft 365 tenant, set up DNS records with your domain registrar, migrate existing email history, configure Teams channels and enable MFA on all accounts before handing over. The whole process typically takes one session for a small team.

For existing Microsoft 365 users on the Gold Coast, the most valuable thing we can do is an audit. We check whether MFA is enabled on all accounts, whether shared mailboxes are configured correctly (a common licencing issue that costs money unnecessarily), whether OneDrive is actually backing up the right folders, and whether any former staff members still have active accounts. Most audits reveal at least one significant issue — usually a security gap or a licencing inefficiency.

Microsoft 365 plans vary in what they include and what they cost. Business Basic gives your team email, Teams and cloud storage — sufficient for most small Gold Coast businesses. Business Standard adds the desktop Office applications (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) on up to 5 devices per user. Business Premium adds advanced security features including Intune for mobile device management and Defender for Business. We advise the right plan for your team size and usage without upselling licences you don’t need.

Microsoft 365 is also the foundation for broader IT improvements. Once email is correctly configured and MFA is enabled, adding cloud backup, managing staff devices through Intune and eventually moving to a fully managed IT arrangement becomes straightforward. For Gold Coast businesses ready to take the next step, Microsoft Copilot can be added to eligible Microsoft 365 plans to bring AI into Teams, Outlook, Word and Excel. We work with Gold Coast businesses at whatever stage they are at — new setup, fixing an existing mess or expanding a well-configured tenant.
