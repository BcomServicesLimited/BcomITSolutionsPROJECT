The first thing we do on arrival is run a full system health check. We look at what’s running in the background, how much of the processor and memory is being used, how full the drive is, and whether the drive itself is healthy. Nothing happens without your understanding and agreement.

Most performance jobs are completed in a single visit. By the time we leave your Gold Coast home, the computer is measurably faster — not just a little quicker, but noticeably different. Startup times drop, programs open faster, and the general sluggishness that made the computer frustrating to use is gone. Slow Computer Situations We See Every Day Across the Gold Coast

One of the most common calls we get from Gold Coast homes is the computer that takes ten minutes to get to the desktop. The homeowner has been putting up with it for months, assuming it’s just old age. In most cases, it’s not. It’s a combination of too many startup programs, a drive that’s nearly full, and Windows Update running in the background every morning. Fix those three things and the same computer starts in under a minute. We see this regularly in Helensvale, Coomera and Nerang, and the result is always the same — the customer can’t believe it’s the same machine.

Overheating is another issue that comes up constantly, particularly with laptops that are three or more years old. Dust builds up inside the vents and around the fan, and the processor starts throttling its own speed to avoid damage. The computer feels slow because it is slow — deliberately, as a self-protection measure. Cleaning the fan and replacing the thermal paste on the processor fixes this completely in most cases. We do this at your home in Broadbeach, Coolangatta, Surfers Paradise or wherever you are on the Gold Coast, without you needing to drop the machine off anywhere.

Browser slowdowns are increasingly common as people accumulate extensions, saved passwords and cached data over years of use. A browser that once opened in two seconds now takes fifteen. Pages load slowly, tabs crash, and video calls stutter. This is almost always a software issue — too many extensions running in the background, a corrupted cache, or a browser that hasn’t been updated in months. We clean it up, remove what doesn’t need to be there, and get the browser back to the speed it should be. Most people in Southport and Burleigh Heads who call us about internet slowness find that the problem is the browser, not the connection.

Hard drive health is something we check on every performance visit, even if it’s not the primary complaint. A drive that’s starting to fail will cause slowdowns, freezes and occasional errors long before it actually dies. Catching it early means the data can be backed up and the drive replaced before anything is lost. We’ve found failing drives on performance visits in Robina and Palm Beach where the customer had no idea there was a hardware problem — they just thought the computer was getting old. Identifying it early made a real difference.
