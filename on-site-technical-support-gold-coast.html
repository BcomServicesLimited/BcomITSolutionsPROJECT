What to Expect From an On-site IT Support Visit

Here's exactly what happens from the moment you call to the moment the fault is resolved.

1

Call and Describe the Fault

Call 07 3041 8993 and describe what's happening — which systems are affected, how many staff are impacted and how long it has been down. We triage on the call — if the fault can be resolved remotely we will tell you before booking a visit. Critical faults are prioritised immediately. Standard faults are scheduled within 1–2 business days.

2

We Confirm Attendance and Arrival Time

We confirm the attending technician, estimated arrival window and full cost before the visit. No surprises on time or fee — call-out fee plus 1-hour increments, agreed upfront. Managed IT customers receive confirmation within the 4-hour SLA.

3

We Arrive and Diagnose On-site

We arrive within the agreed window and work through the fault systematically — hardware, software, network and configuration all checked in a logical sequence. We explain what we find as we go so you're not left wondering what's being done to your systems.

4

Fixed and Confirmed Before We Leave

We test the fix with the affected staff before signing off. If the fault requires parts or a return visit we brief you clearly on next steps, expected timeline and any additional cost — nothing proceeds without your approval.

Critical faults: same-day attendance where possible. Standard faults: typically 1–2 business days. Managed IT customers: 4-hour response time guaranteed.
